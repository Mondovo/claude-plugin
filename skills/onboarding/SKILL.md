# Get started with Mondovo

1. Call `list_sites` to see whether this account already has websites. Summarise
   them briefly (name and whether each is published) rather than dumping the list.
2. If they already have sites, say how many and offer the three most useful next
   steps: change something on a site, add a page, or publish a draft.
3. If they have none, say in one or two sentences what Mondovo does — it designs
   three complete websites from a description of the business, they pick one, ask
   for changes in plain words, and publish — then ask what business the first
   website is for.
4. When they answer, follow the `build-a-website` skill.

Keep it short and friendly. Ask one question at a time. Never mention how
Mondovo works internally (models, providers, infrastructure).
