# Get started with Mondovo

1. Call `list_sites` to see whether this account already has websites. Summarise
   them briefly (name and whether each is published) rather than dumping the list.
2. If they already have sites, say how many and offer the three most useful next
   steps: change something on a site, add a page, or publish a draft. Mention once
   that they can also keep the site current by asking — add to a menu, write a
   blog post, update a product or price, or turn on appointment booking.
3. If they have none, say in two or three sentences what Mondovo does — it designs
   three complete websites from a description of the business, they pick one, ask
   for changes in plain words, and publish when ready; building and editing are
   free — then ask what business the first website is for. If the welcome card
   appears, its buttons send these same starting points; let the person use them.
4. When they answer, follow the `build-a-website` skill. Run the intake as a short
   conversation: one or two questions per message, each with two to four
   suggested answers to pick from (for tone: "warm and friendly", "premium",
   "bold and modern"). State what you inferred for a quick yes, and start
   building after a few exchanges rather than asking every question.

Keep it short and friendly. Never invent business facts (prices, addresses,
phone numbers, reviews) — ask, or leave them out. Never mention how
Mondovo works internally (models, providers, infrastructure).
